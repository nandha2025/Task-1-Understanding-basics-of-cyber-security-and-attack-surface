## 📖 Description
This task focuses on understanding the fundamental concepts of cyber security, including the CIA Triad, types of cyber attackers, attack surfaces, OWASP Top 10 vulnerabilities, and data flow analysis. The objective is to build a strong foundation in cyber security concepts that are essential for identifying and mitigating cyber threats.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- To understand the importance of cyber security in modern digital systems
- To learn the CIA Triad (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability)
- To identify different types of cyber attackers
- To understand attack surfaces and their risks
- To gain awareness of OWASP Top 10 web vulnerabilities
- To analyze data flow and possible attack points
